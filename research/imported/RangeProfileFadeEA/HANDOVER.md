## 2. Rules in plain words (as coded in v1.22)

**Bars.** Everything is computed on closed bars only. When a new bar opens, the EA processes the bar that just closed, then may enter at that moment.

**ATR.** 14-bar simple moving average of true range (MT5 `iATR` method). Wilder RMA is available as an option but was not used in R2 to R6.

**Swings.** A swing high or low is a pivot with 3 bars on each side, confirmed 3 bars after it forms. The last 10 alternating swings are kept (two swings of the same side in a row replace the older one if more extreme).

**Range detection (only when no range is active, cooldown is over and ATR is ready).** The EA looks at the newest N stored swings (N from 4 up to all 10; the minimum is max(2 touches x 2, 3 rotations + 1) = 4). A range is accepted when all hold: at least 2 swing highs and 2 swing lows among them; the highs are within 2 x (0.30 x ATR) of each other and so are the lows (edge stability); range height (average highs minus average lows) is between 1.0 and 6.0 ATR; the swings span at least 8 bars; there are at least 3 rotations (N minus 1 is at least 3). The largest qualifying N is used. Top = average of the swing highs, bottom = average of the swing lows. While active, an edge moves 20% of the way toward any new same-side pivot that lands within 0.30 ATR of it.

**Range ends.** A range is cancelled after 3 consecutive closes outside the same edge (break buffer 0). Swings are cleared and a 4-bar cooldown starts.

**Deviation / signal.** While a range is active, the first close above the top (or below the bottom) opens a deviation and records the highest high (lowest low) since then. The first later close back inside the range [bottom, top] is the signal. Direction is the opposite of the deviation: an upward deviation gives a short, a downward one gives a long. The deviation extreme, ATR and range edges are frozen at that moment.

**Entry.** At the first tick of the next bar, market order at ask (long) or bid (short), only if all of these pass:
- No position is open (one at a time).
- Entry time allowed (see Session).
- The signal bar is in the same trading day as now, and no trade was closed during the signal bar.
- Entry hour in UTC is not 9 (`InpBlockedHoursUTC="9"`, covers 09:00 to 09:59 UTC).
- The stop distance is at least 4 x the current spread (`InpMinRiskSpreads=4.0`).
- Price is not already beyond the stop or target, and the stop and target are outside the broker's stops level.
- Lot size is at or above the minimum lot.

Not used (set to off): trend filter, max trades per day, ATR min/max stop filters, max-spread filter.

**Stop loss.** Deviation extreme plus 0.10 x ATR beyond it (above the high for shorts, below the low for longs). Placed as a real stop on the order.

**Take profit.** Fixed target = entry + 2.0 x risk (risk = entry price to stop distance), placed as a real take-profit on the order.

**Partial exit and break-even (the R5 and R6 change).** The partial price is computed at entry: the range's volume-profile POC (14 rows over the range's bars, tick volume, up to 1,500 bars) if the POC is at least 0.3 x risk in profit from entry and nearer than the target; otherwise entry + 1.25 x risk. When the live price (bid for longs, ask for shorts) reaches it, the EA closes 60% of the lots (rounded down to the lot step) at market, then moves the stop to the entry price (offset 0 points). The remaining 40% runs to the 2R target, the break-even stop, or the session exit. This is done by polling every tick, not by a pending order.

**Position sizing.** 0.5% of current account balance. Lots = (balance x 0.5%) / (loss of 1 lot from entry to stop, from `OrderCalcProfit`), rounded down to the lot step, capped at the symbol maximum. Skipped if below the minimum lot. Risk compounds with balance. Fixed lots are available (`InpFixedLots`) but were not used.

**Session and time zone.** Server time is converted to UTC. In the tester, Auto uses `InpTesterTZ` = GMT+2/+3 with US DST (MetaQuotes-Demo). Rules, all in UTC:
- Positions are closed from 20:55 (21:00 minus 5 minutes) until 23:00, on a new trading day, or 5 minutes before the broker's own daily session end.
- No new entries in the last 30 minutes before 21:00, and none from 21:00 until 23:00 (daily break). On Fridays no entries from 19:00.
- Friday: positions closed from 20:00. No entries on Saturday or on Friday from 21:00.
- Early-close days (Dec 24, Dec 31, Jul 3, Jul 4, US Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday): no entries, positions closed from 16:00.

(21:00 UTC = 02:30 IST.)

## 5. Findings

### 5.1 What worked

- **Deviation fade beats breakout and retest entries** in the early engine sweep (conclusion only; numbers not retained).
- **Time zone and session fixes** took the plain-2R version from R1 (PF 1.10, equity DD 16.35%, max hold 30:45:00) to R4 (PF 1.24, equity DD 7.99%, max hold 29:00:00).
- **Blocking entry hour 9 UTC:** R3 to R4, profit factor 1.20 to 1.24 and net profit 4,529.08 to 5,445.09 on the same 10,000 deposit.
- **Partial exit at POC (else fixed R) plus break-even:** R5 and R6. Win rate rose from about 40% to 58.06% and 56.61%, equity DD fell from 7.99% to 4.50% and 4.00%, max holding time fell to 17:54:20.
- **Best run: R6** (PF 1.39, recovery factor 11.23, equity DD max 6 457.29 (4.00%), 62 of 94 months profitable, long and short PF 1.36 and 1.42). Every year 2019 to 2026 except 2024 was profitable in R6 (2024: net -1,458.37, PF 0.9509, 83 positions).
- Your neighbour checks all had profit factor above 1.2 (reported by you; I did not see them).

### 5.2 What did not work

- **Breakout entries and retest entries at POC/equilibrium:** no edge in the early engine.
- **R1 version:** wrong time zone assumption and weekend/Friday holds, tiny stops. PF 1.10 and 16.35% equity DD.
- **Plain 2R target** (R1 to R4): win rate 37.20% to 40.11% with long losing streaks (up to 18 in R1, 8 in R4).
- Weak years: 2024 is a loss in R5 (PF 0.9656) and R6 (PF 0.9509). 2019 is near break-even in R5 and R6 (R6 2019: net 909.80, PF 1.0579 per the appendix; R5 2020: PF 1.0361). In the plain-2R runs 2020 was a loss (R4 net -240.95, PF 0.9191).

### 5.3 Bugs found and fixed

| Area | Bug | Fix |

Known behaviour, not a bug: MT5 reports partial closes as separate deals, so MT5's "Total Trades" and won-% are inflated versus positions. The partial exit is a market order sent when price touches the level, so live fills will differ from the tester.
